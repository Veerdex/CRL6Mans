CRL 6Mans — Wagers & Prediction System Explainer
==================================================


OVERVIEW
--------
The wagers tab ("Westside Wages") lets players place coin bets on match outcomes.
Odds are derived from a win-probability model built on team ratings. After each
match, team ratings are updated using a zero-sum, series-score-based formula.

There are two separate systems working together:
  1. Player/team rating calculation + win prediction (what odds to show before the game)
  2. Season rating update (how team ratings change after the game)

Model name: crl-final-rating-v1. All the math below lives in app/lib/rating.ts,
shared by the wager predictor (app/dashboard/wagers/prediction.ts) and the
season updater (app/lib/discord-bot.ts) so both interpret a rating gap the
same way.


=======================================
PART 1 — PLAYER RATING
=======================================

File: app/lib/rating.ts (calculatePlayerRating)

Each player supplies six MMR values: all-time peak and season peak for 1v1,
2v2, and 3v3. (1v1 is optional at registration but required before a player
can enter the draft — see enterDraft's gate.)

STEP 1 — AVERAGE EACH PLAYLIST
---------------------------------
  average_1v1 = (peak_1v1 + season_1v1) / 2
  average_2v2 = (peak_2v2 + season_2v2) / 2
  average_3v3 = (peak_3v3 + season_3v3) / 2

STEP 2 — CONVERT 1v1 AND 3v3 ONTO THE 2v2 SCALE
---------------------------------------------------
2v2 is used as-is (it's the reference scale). 1v1 and 3v3 go through their own
degree-5/degree-6 polynomial conversion (fit against real player data, clamped
to a sane input/output range) so all three playlists become comparable:

  convert1v1(x)  — quintic in x, clamped to [100, 1800] in, [100, 2800] out
  convert3v3(x)  — degree-6 in normalized t = (x-100)/2100, same output clamp

STEP 3 — WEIGHTED POWER MEAN ACROSS PLAYLISTS
--------------------------------------------------
Each converted playlist value gets a direct multiplier and a playlist weight,
then they're combined with a power mean (p = 10, i.e. strongly biased toward
the player's best playlist rather than a plain average):

  DIRECT_MULTIPLIERS = { 1v1: 0.868, 2v2: 1.0, 3v3: 0.625 }
  PLAYLIST_WEIGHTS   = { 1v1: 0.423, 2v2: 0.531, 3v3: 1.0 }

  playerRating = powerMean(
    [convert1v1(avg1v1)*0.868, avg2v2*1.0, convert3v3(avg3v3)*0.625],
    weights = [0.423, 0.531, 1.0],
    p = 10
  )

Typical player ratings land in the ~1000-1800 range.


=======================================
PART 2 — TEAM RATING
=======================================

File: app/lib/rating.ts (initialTeamRating)

A CRL team is exactly three players. Given the three players' ratings:

STEP 1 — BASE RATING (power mean, p ≈ 11.4)
------------------------------------------------
  baseRating = powerMean([r1, r2, r3], weights=[1,1,1], p=11.406)

(Short rosters are padded with the roster's own average; long rosters keep
only the top 3 by rating.)

STEP 2 — CARRY-GAP BONUS
----------------------------
A team carried hard by one strong player is slightly stronger than the power
mean alone suggests (the strong player can take over more games). Sort the
three ratings descending as [strongest, second, weakest]:

  carryGap = strongest - (second + weakest) / 2
  teamRating = baseRating + 0.199 * carryGap

Used both as the fallback team rating (when a team has no season_rating yet)
and as the reference point for teamRatingDeltaFromRatingChange, which computes
the marginal effect of a single roster change (e.g. an approved MMR edit)
without needing to re-derive the whole season history.


=======================================
PART 3 — PER-GAME WIN PROBABILITY
=======================================

File: app/lib/rating.ts (winProbability)

Given two team ratings (either from teams.season_rating or a freshly computed
initialTeamRating), the probability that team A wins a single game is a flat
logistic curve:

  P(A wins 1 game) = 1 / (1 + 10^((ratingB - ratingA) / 300))

This is calibrated as a genuine per-game probability (verified against the
CPS evaluator, which scores it directly against raw per-game share), so it
feeds straight into the series-probability math below with no extra scaling.

Example: ratingA = 1500, ratingB = 1400 → P ≈ 63%


=======================================
PART 4 — SERIES WIN PROBABILITY & ODDS
=======================================

File: app/dashboard/wagers/prediction.ts

STEP 1 — SERIES WIN PROBABILITY (negative binomial)
-------------------------------------------------------
For a Best-of-N series, winsNeeded = ceil(N / 2). Sum over all ways to win:
win the last game, and lose anywhere from 0 to (winsNeeded - 1) prior games.

  P(series win) = SUM over losses in [0, winsNeeded - 1]:
    C(winsNeeded + losses - 1, losses)
    * perGameP ^ winsNeeded
    * (1 - perGameP) ^ losses

STEP 2 — OVER/UNDER PROBABILITIES
--------------------------------------
The model enumerates every possible game scoreline (home wins W, home losses
L) for all W,L combos that produce a valid series result, sums probability
mass by total game count T = W + L, then slices that distribution at each
Over/Under line (e.g. 2.5, 3.5, 4.5 for BO5) to get Over%/Under%.

STEP 3 — ODDS / PAYOUT MULTIPLIER
--------------------------------------
A 5% house vig is applied:

  HOUSE_VIG = 0.05
  payoutMultiplier(prob) = round((0.95 / prob) * 100) / 100

The multiplier is displayed in the UI as American-style odds as well:
  If multiplier >= 2.0: odds = +(multiplier - 1) * 100   (underdog, e.g. +150)
  If multiplier < 2.0:  odds = -(100 / (multiplier - 1)) (favorite, e.g. -200)

STEP 4 — PARLAY BETS
-------------------------
Parlay legs use the same odds multiplier per leg. The combined parlay
multiplier is the product of all individual leg multipliers; all legs must
win for the parlay to pay out.


===============================================
PART 5 — SEASON RATING UPDATE (POST-MATCH)
===============================================

File: app/lib/rating.ts (applyRatingUpdate), app/lib/discord-bot.ts
      (applySeasonRatingUpdate)

INITIALIZATION (first match only)
-------------------------------------
If a team's season_rating is null (hasn't played), it's initialized from its
current roster via initialTeamRating (Part 2).

STEP 1 — SERIES SCORE SHARE
--------------------------------
Rather than a binary win/loss, the "actual" result is a compressed share of
games won in the series (SERIES_SCORE_EXPONENT = 0.335 — a 2-0 sweep counts
for more than a 2-1 win, but not twice as much):

  updateShareA = gamesWonA^0.335 / (gamesWonA^0.335 + gamesWonB^0.335)

STEP 2 — RATING DELTA (zero-sum)
-------------------------------------
  probabilityA = winProbability(ratingA, ratingB)     — Part 3
  deltaA       = EFFECTIVE_K * (updateShareA - probabilityA)
  newRatingA   = ratingA + deltaA
  newRatingB   = ratingB - deltaA

  EFFECTIVE_K = BASE_K (35) * UPDATE_MULTIPLIER (2.25) = 78.75

Whatever one team gains, the other loses exactly — no rating is created or
destroyed by a match result. An outperforming underdog (winning more/bigger
than its win probability implied) gains rating; a favorite that barely
squeaks by loses a little instead of gaining.

FORFEITS ARE A COMPLETE NO-OP
----------------------------------
If a match is reported as a forfeit, the games never happened — the rating
update is skipped entirely for both teams, regardless of any other admin
"skip rating update" flag.

STEP 3 — FINAL WRITE
-------------------------
Both home and away teams get their season_rating updated simultaneously via
Supabase. The next time odds are computed for either team, the new rating is
used as the starting point.


==================================
PART 6 — BET RESOLUTION (COINS)
==================================

File: app/lib/discord-bot.ts  (resolveMatchWagers, voidMatchWagers)
      app/dashboard/wagers/actions.ts  (placeBets, placeParlayBet)

HOW COINS FLOW:

Placing a bet:
  - crl_coins is debited immediately on bet placement
  - Status set to "pending"

When match result is reported (execReportMatchResult):
  - Rating update runs first (applySeasonRatingUpdate), skipped entirely on forfeit
  - Then wager resolution fires asynchronously:
    - Winning bets: increment_crl_coins(player_id, round(amount * odds_multiplier))
    - Losing bets: no coins returned (already debited at placement)
    - Parlays: only pay if ALL legs won; payout = round(amount * combined_multiplier)

On forfeit / DQ (forfeit=true in execReportMatchResult):
  - voidMatchWagers runs instead
  - All bets on that match are marked "void"
  - Original stakes are refunded via increment_crl_coins

BET TYPES (stored in wagers.bet_type):
  - "home" / "away"         — series winner
  - "over_X.X" / "under_X.X" — total games played O/U at a given line


RATING DOES NOT AFFECT COINS DIRECTLY.
Player crl_coins only change through bet outcomes. The season_rating on teams
is separate and only affects what odds future bets receive.
